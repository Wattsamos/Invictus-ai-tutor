**WHAT IS A TOKEN?**



**AI models generally don't read text exactly like humans do. What they process is a token.**



**A token can a word or part of a word or punctuation mark or a special symbol.**





**WHAT IS TOKENIZER**

**It converts human readable text into the format the model can process**
